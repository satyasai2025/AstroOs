First v2 Milestone

I recommend defining Milestone M1 as:

"First End-to-End Astrology Pipeline"

Success criteria:

Enter birth details.
Generate D1 chart.
Generate all required Vargas.
Compute Dashas.
Detect Yogas.
Apply Rule Engine.
Query Knowledge Office.
Correlate Research Data.
Produce cited report.
Validate against Benchmark specifications.

This gives you a complete vertical slice of AstroOS rather than isolated components.

Before coding v2

I recommend one final administrative step:

Commit the current repository state.
Tag it as:
v1.0.0-foundation

That creates a clean baseline. From there, all new work belongs to the AstroOS v2 roadmap, and your governance offices can continue supporting implementation through formal requests rather than expanding their own foundational artifacts.
