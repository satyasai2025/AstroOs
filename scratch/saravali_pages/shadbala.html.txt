Shadbala
Shadbala is a powerful source for calculation of planetary strength. It
is described in chapter 27 of Hora Shastra. Shadbala consists of 6
different sources of strength. Each source represents an aspect of
strength, such as planetary placement in zodiacal signs or aspectual
strength.
