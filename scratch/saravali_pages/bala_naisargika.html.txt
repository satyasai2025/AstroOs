Naisargika Bala
Naisargika Bala is the natural strength of the planets. This strength
does not depend on the specific chart but is fixed for all charts.
Signification
Planets with nearly identical Shadbala can be evaluated by their
Naisargika Bala, i.e. the planet with higher Naisargika Bala can be
regarded as stronger.
Calculation
The seven bodies are ordered by their luminosity and get relative
strength according to their position in that order. Sun has the highest
luminosity, Saturn is the darkest planet; other planets get portions of
1/7 Rupa according to their position in that order.
